Why BeReal is my new favorite app

Five nonnegotiables for an ethical social media app

Op-ed

The first time I downloaded BeReal, it only lasted a few days. I had downloaded it to see what the fuss was all about, but after five days, I got bored. When one of my friends asked me why I had deleted it, I said half-jokingly, “It wasn’t addicting enough.”

She laughed and responded, “Isn’t that the point?” I had to admit, she had me there.

What is the point of having social media? To be addicted? I had gotten so used to how social media apps like Instagram and TikTok served me in my life – constantly and mind-numbingly – that I forgot how social media should play a role in my life – momentarily and forgettable.

The fact that I could delete the app on my own accord made me think, hey… maybe this app isn’t so bad. So, maybe a bit ironically, I redownloaded it, and to this day, I take two minutes a day to take a photo, and maybe another two minutes to look at my friends’ photos, but that’s it. No doom-scrolling. No falling down a rabbit hole. Just a moment to capture and share. And I could delete it today if I wanted to.

Typical modern social media apps are designed to get us addicted, steal our time, and convince us that we need things that we don’t. And we’ve normalized it to the point that its “addictiveness” is the standard of whether an app is “good” or not. However, a “good” app doesn’t mean that it’s good for us. We need social media apps that have our best interests at heart and aren’t trying to prey on our downfall. We need social media apps that are ethical.

Here’s my list of nonnegotiables for an ethical social media app:

#1 Has limits

BeReal has two types of limits: quantity and time. One of the most interesting features of BeReal is that you can only take and post less than 5 photos per day, and you only have two minutes to capture each photo.

#2 Not self-regulated

You don’t get to tell BeReal when to post. BeReal tells you when to post.

#3 Simple

Take a photo from your front and back camera and upload. Easy as that.

#4 No DMs

You can only comment on BeReal. One of the reasons I can never delete Instagram is because a lot of communication happens through Instagram DMs, especially with acquaintances or classmates who you aren’t close enough to text.

#5 Authentic

I can’t say this with 100% certainty because there are definitely people who plan out and pose their BeReals. However, the whole point of BeReal is to be spontaneous and show yourself doing whatever you’re doing at that moment in time. Hence, the name BE REAL. It challenges the standard notions of social media filters, photoshop editing, and only showing the highlights of your life. It’s just you at one moment going about your day. It’s unglamorous. It’s simple. It’s unguarded. And it’s wonderful.

It’s apps like BeReal that have restored my faith in social media companies. With companies like Meta and TikTok treating their users like money objects, it’s refreshing to see how a smaller app like BeReal can bring back a little bit of our humanity.

At the end of the day, I still believe that people will choose their freedom and humanity over convenience, entertainment, and addiction – and that is the reason why ethical apps like BeReal will survive in the market.
