Connexpedition 2024 Application Post

Wisdom Culture & Education Organization · 2024

Connexpedition (often shortened to Connex) is an international youth service program for high school students in the San Francisco Bay Area. Created by Wisdom Culture & Education Organization in 2011, each year, Connex selects and trains a group of high school volunteers to travel to Taiwan and teach a 2-week-long English summer camp in New Taipei City. There, volunteers will teach English, share about American culture, and get to explore the traditions, lifestyles, and values of the island nation.

⭐️MORE INFO⭐️

  • Applications open January 1, 2024, through February 11, 2024
  • Online group interviews January 26, 2024, through February 17, 2024
  • Trainings from March 2024 through June 2024
  • Summer service trip to Taiwan June 27, 2024, through July 13, 2024

⭐️ELIGIBILITY⭐️

  • Applicants must reside within the San Francisco Bay Area and be able to travel to in-person orientation and training sessions.
  • Applicants must be enrolled in grades 9-12 at the time of application.
  • Applicants must be holders of a passport eligible for a visa (or visa-exempt) that allows them to stay in Taiwan for the duration of the entire program.
  • Applicants must be able to demonstrate an intermediate to advanced proficiency in Mandarin Chinese during the interview process.

Learn more about our program and how to apply at our website: connexpedition.com/apply
